# Vegetation asset licenses

The vegetation models in this directory are optimized derivatives of assets
published by Poly Haven under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 license.

| Project asset | Source | Authors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `tree_a` | [Tree Small 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02) | Rico Cilliers |
| `tree_b` | [Fir Sapling](https://polyhaven.com/a/fir_sapling) | Rob Tuytel, Rico Cilliers |
| `grass` | [Grass Medium 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_01) | Rob Tuytel, Rico Cilliers |

License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

The source geometry was reduced with glTF Transform 4.4.2. The supplied 1K
textures were retained. Attribution is not required by CC0, but the source
credits are kept here for traceability.
